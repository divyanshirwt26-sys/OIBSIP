01 / THE MAN BEHIND THE VISION

From humble beginnings
to national inspiration.

Born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam grew up with a passion for learning and scientific discovery. He studied physics and aerospace engineering.

His career included significant work at DRDO and ISRO, including India's Satellite Launch Vehicle programme. He later served as India's 11th President from 2002 to 2007, becoming widely known as the People's President.
